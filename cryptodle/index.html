0xCRYPTODLE

Guess today's member of the Aarhus crypto group, past or present.

    Data from public pages (group site, personal pages, IACR ePrint), checked . Daily resets at midnight, Aarhus time.

    How to play

    Every day there is a new mystery person — a professor, postdoc, PhD student, alumnus or visitor of the Aarhus crypto group. Same puzzle for everyone.

    Classic

    Guess anyone. Each column compares your guess with the target:

    correct partial overlap / same family wrong ▲ the target's value is higher
    Role
    Current position. Orange = same family (faculty · researcher · student).
    At Aarhus
    Current member, former member, visiting now, former visitor.
    Aarhus path
    What they have been at Aarhus: student, PhD, postdoc, faculty, visitor, staff.
    Research
    Main topics (up to three).
    Based in
    Country of the current employer.
    PhD from
    Country of the PhD institution (or where the PhD is in progress).
    PhD year
    Year obtained. “Ongoing” counts as later than any year.
    ePrints
    Distinct IACR ePrint deposits.

    ePrint

    Read a real ePrint title and name one of its authors from the group. Orange means your guess has co-authored with one of the authors. Hints unlock as you go.
